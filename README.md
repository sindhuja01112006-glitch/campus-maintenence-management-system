Campus Maintenance Management System

A Campus Maintenance Management System (CMMS) is a web/mobile-based system that allows students and faculty to report campus problems, such as broken lights, damaged furniture, water leakage, electrical problems, or sanitation issues. Maintenance staff can view, assign, update, and resolve these complaints, while administrators monitor the overall maintenance process.

1. Problem Statement

In many colleges, maintenance complaints are reported verbally or through registers. This can lead to:

Complaints being lost or forgotten
Delays in solving problems
Difficulty tracking complaint status
No proper record of previous maintenance
Lack of accountability for maintenance staff
Difficulty for administrators to monitor pending issues

The proposed system provides a centralized digital platform to manage all campus maintenance complaints.

2. Main Objectives
Allow students and faculty to report maintenance issues online.
Allow users to upload an image of the problem.
Record the location of the issue.
Automatically categorize complaints.
Assign complaints to appropriate maintenance staff.
Track complaint status.
Notify users when the complaint is updated or resolved.
Provide an admin dashboard for monitoring.
Maintain a history of all maintenance activities.
Generate reports about maintenance performance.
